# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy
research community.

## Expected behaviour

- demonstrate empathy, patience, and respect;
- give and receive specific, constructive feedback;
- distinguish critique of evidence or method from criticism of a person;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct the record, and learn from them;
- respect confidentiality, data licences, authorship discussions, and research participants;
- focus on the integrity of the project and the wellbeing of its collaborators.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, sexualised language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, intimidation, sustained disruption, and publishing
another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project.
Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions
that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report an incident privately to a repository administrator or the Anthropocene Laboratory
project lead through an established institutional channel. External contributors who do
not have a private contact route may open an issue titled **“Request private maintainer
contact”** without including incident details; a maintainer will arrange a confidential
channel. Do not report sensitive conduct details in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers handling a report must protect
the reporter's privacy and disclose information only as necessary for a fair response.
